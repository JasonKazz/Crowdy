# ZIP density counting

The reviewed ZIP-N inference implementation in `app/density_model.py` is adapted
from [Yiming-M/ZIP](https://github.com/Yiming-M/ZIP), revision
`bb52b33151ed9a5a31d46ef47513b2ceeaf6de29`. The published checkpoint comes from
the author's [ZIP-N model repository](https://huggingface.co/Yiming-M/ZIP-N).
The paper is [ZIP: Scalable Crowd Counting via Zero-Inflated Poisson
Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19955).
